# Features

Complete feature list for POS Management System

- User authentication and registration (ASP.NET Identity)
- Role management and role assignment
- Product management with image upload
- Category management
- Supplier management
- Customer management
- Stock In (purchase / inbound stock) with supplier payments
- Stock Out (sales / outbound stock) with customer payments
- Low stock alerts (background job via Hangfire) sending email notifications
- Reports: Sales report with Excel export, other report pages
- Dashboard with KPIs and monthly totals
- Server-side validation with Data Annotations and CSRF protection
